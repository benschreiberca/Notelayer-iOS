Voice input, task hierarchy, and full analytics are now available to everyone — no feature gates, no toggles required.

New 4-step walkthrough gets you adding tasks in under a minute. Choose your category preset, add your first task, and go.

## Full Description

```
Notelayer makes it easy to capture, organize, and analyze your tasks — in one beautiful app built for how you actually think.

CAPTURE YOUR IDEAS
• Add tasks with a single tap
• Voice capture for hands-free entry
• Assign tasks to multiple categories at once
• Set priorities, due dates, and reminders

ORGANIZE YOUR LIFE
• See tasks by category, priority, or due date — or view everything at once
• Multi-category tasks (one task, many areas)
• Parent-child task hierarchy for bigger projects
• Keep your most important work front and center

ANALYZE YOUR PATTERNS
• Insights tab shows your completion rate, streaks, and productivity trends
• Category usage breakdown tells you where your time and energy is going
• Oldest open tasks helps you find your long-hanging fruit
• Time-of-day usage patterns show when you're most productive
• Deep-link from Insights directly to the relevant To-Dos view

GET STARTED IN SECONDS
A 4-step guided onboarding experience walks you through setting up your categories and adding your first task. Choose Everyday Balance, Life Admin, or Growth & Projects — or start blank. You'll be organized in under a minute.

MORE FEATURES
• Calendar export: add tasks with due dates directly to your Apple Calendar
• Reminders: get notified when tasks are due
• Themes: light mode, dark mode, accent colors, and surface styles to match your aesthetic
• Share extension: capture content from any app directly into Notelayer
• Background sync across your devices

Notelayer is built for people who want a task manager that stays out of their way — and shows them something useful when they check in.

Download and get organized today.
```
