# Marketing Brief — Notelayer 1.5.0

Angles, social copy, and blog/email hooks for the v1.5.0 release.

---

## Release Theme

**"Finally, a task app that shows you what matters."**

v1.5.0 is the "clarity" release — fewer tabs, no gates, better first-time experience, and Insights that actually lead somewhere. The through-line: everything is simpler and more intentional.

---

## Key Angles

### 1. Simplicity
"We removed a tab, a toggle, and a lot of friction. Notelayer is now exactly what you need and nothing else."

### 2. Insights → Action
"Most analytics tools show you data. Notelayer shows you data and then takes you to the thing you can actually do about it."

### 3. Onboarding that respects your time
"Get set up in under 60 seconds. One idea per screen, no video to watch, no endless forms. Add a task and go."

### 4. Everything is standard now
"Voice input. Task hierarchy. Full analytics. No experimental flags. All features, all users, version 1.5.0."

---

## Social Copy

**Twitter / X (short):**
> Notelayer 1.5 is out. Streamlined to 2 tabs. All features now standard. New onboarding that gets you organized in 60 seconds. Insights that link you to action. Worth a look.

**Instagram caption:**
> v1.5.0 is live. Cleaner navigation, fully redesigned onboarding, and Insights that don't just show you data — they take you somewhere with it. Tap your category in Insights → jump straight to your To-Dos. Available now on the App Store.

**LinkedIn (professional angle):**
> Shipped Notelayer 1.5.0 today. The headline: removed the experimental features gate entirely. Voice input, task hierarchy, and full analytics are now standard for every user. Also redesigned onboarding from scratch — Duolingo-inspired, interactive, one idea per screen. Goal was to get a new user from zero to their first task in under 60 seconds. The Insights update adds deep-links so tapping a category or a task takes you directly to To-Dos. Less friction, more signal.

## Blog / Email Hook

**Subject:** What we shipped in Notelayer 1.5.0 (and why it matters)

**Hook paragraph:**

A bunch of conditional logic is gone from the codebase. In its place: a simpler app that does more, a faster onboarding flow, and analytics that actually help you take action. Here's what changed.
