# MARKETING 01: Brand Persona and Identity

Last Updated: 2026-04-20
Status: Draft
Type: Brand Foundation
Priority: High — must be defined before other marketing materials

## Purpose

Define who Notelayer is as a brand — the personality, voice, values, and visual identity — so that all marketing materials, social content, and product copy feel coherent and intentional.

## Why Now

Marketing is starting. Without a defined brand persona, every piece of content will feel inconsistent. A clear persona makes content creation faster and gives Notelayer a recognizable presence.

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Productivity-conscious individuals who feel their current to-do app is either too simple or too complex. They manage work and personal tasks in the same mental space and want something that helps them see patterns, not just check boxes.

**Secondary:** Developers and knowledge workers who appreciate well-designed tools and are willing to pay a small amount for something genuinely useful.

**Not targeting:** Enterprise teams, students looking for free tools, people happy with Apple Reminders.

## Brand Personality (Draft)

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Calm | Not urgent or alarm-triggering. Notelayer reduces anxiety, not adds to it. |
| Intelligent | Surfaces patterns and insights. Knows things without being asked. |
| Personal | Built for one person's life — not a team board. |
| Minimal | Never cluttered. Every feature earns its place. |
| Honest | No dark patterns, no engagement traps, no badge counts for their own sake. |

## Voice and Tone

- **Direct:** Say the thing. No marketing fluff.
- **Warm but not cheerful:** Supportive, not peppy.
- **Smart but not technical:** Can explain AI features without jargon.
- **Brief:** If it takes more than 10 words, cut it in half.

### Examples

| Don't say | Say instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| "Supercharge your productivity with AI-powered task intelligence!" | "See what your tasks are actually telling you." |
| "The ultimate to-do list for busy professionals." | "A task manager that pays attention." |
| "Get more done in less time!" | "Know what matters. Do it." |

## Tagline Options (Pick One)

1. "A task manager that pays attention."
2. "Know what matters. Do it."
3. "Your tasks. Your patterns. Your insights."
4. "Task management with a memory."

**Recommendation:** Option 1 — it positions Notelayer as active/intelligent without being hyperbolic.

## Visual Identity Notes

- Logo: existing Notelayer logo — confirm it works at small sizes (app icon, social avatar).
- Color: a single brand accent color distinct from iOS defaults. Should work in both light and dark contexts.
- Typography: clean sans-serif; system font (SF Pro) is acceptable for product UI, but marketing materials may benefit from a distinct display typeface.
- Photography / illustration style: abstract, minimal — not stock photos of people at desks.
