# MARKETING 02: LinkedIn Content Strategy

Last Updated: 2026-04-20
Status: Draft
Type: Marketing Plan
Channel: LinkedIn
Priority: Medium

## Purpose

Use LinkedIn to build an audience for Notelayer by sharing the story of building it as a side project — authentically, not as polished marketing copy. The goal is to attract early adopters, get feedback, and establish credibility as a solo founder.

## Positioning on LinkedIn

You are not a company. You are a developer who built something you wanted to exist, made it real, and are now sharing it. This is more compelling than corporate product announcements.

- Lead with the builder story, not the product features.
- Be specific about what you built and why — not generic "productivity" language.
- Share things that did not work, not just wins.

## Content Pillars

### 1. Build-in-Public Updates
- "I shipped X today."
- "I ran into this problem and solved it this way."
- "Here is what I got wrong the first time."
- Frequency: 1–2x per week while actively building.

### 2. Product Milestones
- App Store launch announcement.
- Android launch.
- First 100 users.
- First paying subscriber.
- These are event-driven, not scheduled.

### 3. Product Insight Posts
- "Why I built the analytics the way I did."
- "The one thing I wish every task manager did."
- "How I use Notelayer every day — here is my actual setup."
- Frequency: 1–2x per month.

### 4. Engagement / Questions
- "What is the thing your task manager doesn't do that you wish it did?"
- "How many apps do you use to manage your tasks? Serious question."
- These build comments and reach without requiring content creation.
- Frequency: occasional, when natural.

## Post Format Guidelines

- **Length:** 3–7 short paragraphs. No walls of text. No bullet-list dumps.
- **Hook line:** The first line must make someone stop scrolling. State the most interesting thing upfront.
- **No hashtag spam:** 1–3 relevant hashtags maximum (#buildinpublic #productivity #indiedev).
- **Images/video:** Screenshot of the app or a short screen recording when relevant. Do not post text-only for every post.
- **No AI-generated filler:** Write in your own voice. AI can help edit, not draft from scratch.

## Sample First Post (Draft)

> I've been building a task manager on nights and weekends for the past year.
>
> Not because there aren't enough task managers — there are hundreds. I built it because I wanted one that actually showed me patterns in how I work, not just a list of things to check off.
>
> It's called Notelayer. It's live on the App Store now.
>
> Here's what I learned building it — and what I got wrong along the way. [thread or link]

## Metrics to Track

- Follower growth (weekly).
- Post impressions (per post).
- App Store referral traffic from LinkedIn (via UTM parameters on any shared links).
- Direct messages from potential users.
